Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gem Cashout (the “Game”), published by Volodymyr Alkhymenko (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play the Game on your Android device.

In short

  • The Game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads from third-party advertising partners, which may collect the device information described below.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

1Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following data locally so it can work: level progress and stars, your best score, collection and vault progress, daily gift and daily level status (including the dates you claimed them), and your settings (sound, music, vibration). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2Information collected by third-party services

The Game may use third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising and, in the future, basic analytics and crash reporting. When these services are active, they may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (for example, the Google Advertising ID) and, where available, other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction and usage information (ads shown, ads clicked, session length, game events);
  • Diagnostic and crash information.

This information is collected and used by those providers according to their own privacy policies. We do not control it. Providers we use or may use include:

  • Unity Technologies / LevelPlay (ad mediation and ad networks): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play services / AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

3How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to run the Game, show rewarded and other advertisements, understand and improve the Game, fix errors, and keep the Game secure. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for money. We do not use your information to make decisions that significantly affect you.

4Advertising and your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads: on your Android device go to Settings → Privacy (or Google → Ads) and choose “Delete advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization”. Exact menu names vary by device.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and similar regions): where required by law, the Game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is shown, and you can change your choice later. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Vibration, sound and music can be switched off in the Game’s settings.

5Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us so we can help, and contact the relevant third-party provider where applicable.

6Data retention and deletion

Data stored in the Game stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. We do not hold a server-side copy of your progress. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their policies; please contact them directly to exercise your rights over that data.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not collect data that identifies you directly, most of these requests are best addressed to the relevant advertising provider listed above. You can also contact us and we will do our best to help.

8Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Game and the limited information it handles. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9International transfers

Our advertising partners may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
